## Illustration palette

`src/components/nuru/art/palette.ts` — Shared flat-illustration palette: wine,
terracotta, saffron, sage, plum, cream, skin tones. Original (in-house), CC0.

## Icons

`src/components/nuru/icons/TopicIcon.tsx` — Custom topic icon set, 2px stroke,
rounded ends, brand colors. Original (in-house), CC0.

`src/components/nuru/icons/BadgeIcons.tsx` — Custom badge icon set:
`HandsIcon` (community hands, lived-experience layer), `StethoscopeIcon`
(clinical-response layer), `BookCheckIcon` (evidence-card layer). 2px stroke,
rounded ends, brand colors. Original (in-house), CC0.

## Pattern dividers

`src/components/nuru/PatternDivider.tsx` — Kente, mudcloth, Ndebele, kanga
inspired patterns. Original (in-house), inspired by African textile heritage
but not copying any specific protected design. CC0 (NuruWomen).

## Photos

No photographic assets currently used. All imagery is SVG illustration.
If photos are added later, they must be CC0 or properly licensed, with
dignified, hopeful everyday scenes of African women — never pity or
aid-poster imagery, and never implying a real user, testimonial, or verified
professional.
