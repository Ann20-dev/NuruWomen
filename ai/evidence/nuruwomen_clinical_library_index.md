# NuruWomen Clinical Evidence Seed Library

Compiled: 2 October 2026

Selection criteria: publication year 2020 or later; preference for systematic reviews, meta-analyses, clinical reviews, guideline syntheses and major evidence-based position statements; preference for open-access and Africa/LMIC relevance.

## Ingestion guidance

- Keep `topic`, `year`, `evidence_type`, `PMID/PMCID`, `DOI`, and source URL as metadata.
- Chunk by headings/sections rather than arbitrary character counts where possible.
- Store the citation with every chunk so generated health answers can cite the underlying evidence.
- Give higher retrieval weight to systematic reviews, meta-analyses, guideline syntheses, and current clinical guidance.
- Do not turn population-level evidence into a personal diagnosis. Escalate red-flag or emergency symptoms to professional care.
